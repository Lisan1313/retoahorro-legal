Reto Ahorro privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Leer en español

In short: Reto Ahorro does not collect any data about you. Everything you tick stays stored on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no servers of ours, and we do not send your information anywhere.

What is stored and where

The app stores, only on your phone's storage, what you do in it:

  • Your savings challenges: the type of challenge, the currency, the amounts and the start date.
  • The weeks, envelopes or days you tick as saved, with the date you ticked them.
  • Your preferences: light or dark theme, animations and the reminder time.

The app does not send this information anywhere: not to us and not to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it, so we have no access to it.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any personal data.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign-up.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We do not share, sell or hand over data to third parties, because we do not have any.
  • We do not move money or connect to your bank: the app only keeps track of what you decide to set aside.

Backups

The app lets you create a backup file with your challenges so you don't lose them when you change phones. You create that file and you decide what to do with it: keep it on your phone, in your cloud or send it to yourself. The app does not upload it anywhere on its own. Once you save it in another service, that service's terms apply.

Android's automatic backup. Separately, Android has its own backup. If it is turned on in your phone's settings, it may save this app's data to your Google account and restore it if you reinstall the app or change phones. This is an Android feature, not ours: that backup goes to your account, not to us, and we cannot see it. If you prefer it not to happen, you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only if you turn on the reminder in Settings. The reminder is scheduled by your own phone; it does not go through any server.
  • Files: only when you tap to save or restore a backup, through the system file picker.
  • Vibration: a short vibration when you tick or celebrate. Nothing is stored.

Google Play

  • Purchases: if you buy "All Challenges", the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your card details. Google only tells us whether the purchase is active for your account.
  • Updates: when you open the app, it asks Google Play whether there is a new version so it can let you know. Google Play handles that check.

What Google does with that data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, whatever their age.

How to delete your data

There are two ways, both immediate and permanent:

  • Uninstall the app: everything it stored on the phone is deleted.
  • From Android settings: Apps → Reto Ahorro → Storage → Clear data.

There is no need to ask us for anything, because we do not have any copy.

Changes to this policy

If the app changed and started handling data differently, we would update this page and say so in the app itself before it happens.
